## 🪐 Overview  
ELAIZ is the native token of the **Elaienz ecosystem** — a privacy-focused messenger and a suite of encrypted products designed for confidential communication and secure digital experiences.

The token is immutable, transparent, and permanently fixed at a **15,500,000 supply**.
