# Elaienz / ELAIZ Roadmap

This roadmap reflects the current vision for the Elaienz ecosystem and the ELAIZ token.  
Timing and priorities may evolve as the project grows and the market changes.

---

## ✅ Q4 2025 — Foundation (Completed)

- Deployment of the **ELAIZ** smart contract on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)  
- Fixed supply set to **15,500,000 ELAIZ** (no minting, no admin keys)  
- Token verification and publication on **BscScan**  
- Launch of the official **token page**: `https://elaienz.com/token.php`  
- Creation of the public **GitHub repository** with contract source and docs  
- Submission of **CMC listing request** (Ticket: 1296092)  
- Submission of token info update to **BscScan**  
- Internal integration tests between Elaienz Messenger and the ELAIZ token

---

## 🎯 Q1 2026 — Listings & Transparency

- CMC listing approval for **ELAIZ** (subject to CoinMarketCap review)  
- Submission for additional aggregators (CoinGecko and others)  
- Publication of an expanded **Tokenomics** document with allocation details  
- Public release of the **technical documentation** for ELAIZ integration  
- First wave of community channels:
  - Dedicated token updates in Telegram `@ElaienzOff`
  - Regular devlogs on X (Twitter) `@Elaienzoff`

---

## 🚀 Q2 2026 — Ecosystem Integration

- First **in-app integration** of ELAIZ inside Elaienz Messenger:
  - Premium privacy settings
  - Customization & cosmetic upgrades
- Launch of **subscription model** payable in ELAIZ  
- Internal **developer API** for third-party modules using ELAIZ as a payment layer  
- Initial **partner pilots** for external services accepting ELAIZ within the Elaienz ecosystem

---

## 🧩 Q3 2026 — Mini-Apps & Services

- Launch of the first **mini-apps** inside Elaienz (extensions powered by ELAIZ)  
- Introduction of **service marketplace**:
  - Add-ons, modules, bots and integrations paid in ELAIZ  
- Basic **reward mechanics** for early community contributors (non-custodial, transparent)  
- UX improvements for token interactions:
  - One-tap actions with ELAIZ
  - Clear on-chain status indicators in the UI

---

## 🗳 Q4 2026 — Governance & Community Layer

- Design and publication of the **governance model** for ELAIZ holders  
- Launch of **governance beta**:
  - Non-binding community polls  
  - Signaling proposals for feature priorities and roadmap items  
- Open feedback loops with power-users and community builders  
- Preparation for future DAO-like structure (without rushing into unsafe mechanics)

---

## 🌐 2027 and Beyond — Scaling the Elaienz Ecosystem

- Expansion of Elaienz into a broader **privacy-first product suite**
  - Additional apps and tools built around secure communication  
- Deeper **cross-integration** with Web3:
  - Wallets, bridges, third-party dApps where ELAIZ can be used  
- Continuous **security reviews** and at least one external smart-contract audit published in `/docs/AUDIT.md`  
- Long-term focus on:
  - Sustainable token utility  
  - Transparent development  
  - Community-driven evolution of the ecosystem

---

> This roadmap is not a financial promise or guarantee.  
> It describes the current direction of the project and may be adjusted as Elaienz grows.
